From job posting to tailored PDF

One local project gives your AI assistant the workflow and instructions it needs.

01

Bring your master resume

Use your resume as the source for your experience and, where supported, its visual design. Your assistant guides you through setup.

02

Share the full job posting

Copy the job page text, including the role and requirements. The assistant can pick out the relevant job details.

03

Review the tailored PDF

TailorCV builds the PDF locally. Check every claim and detail before you use it for an application.

Simple to start. Yours to review.

TailorCV brings the project instructions and local build tools together.

⌘

Choose your assistant

Use Claude Code or Codex. The visual guide explains how to install, launch and connect either one.

▤

Build PDFs on your computer

TailorCV uses Node.js and LibreOffice. Your resume files stay in your local project unless you share them with your AI provider.

↗

Keep one reusable master

Set up your base resume once, then use the same project for different job descriptions.

✓

Open source and free

The TailorCV project is MIT licensed. Claude Code or Codex account access and usage are separate.
